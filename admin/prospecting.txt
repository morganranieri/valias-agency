Goal: meet with 5 extra-curricular providers who each have more than 10 tutors 

What can I offer? 
Coffee.  Warmth.  Database.  Admin, tutor, school and public interfaces.  Local support.  Hosting.  

What would I like to know? 
- how many tutors do you have? 
- how many students do you teach
- do you take payment from parents?
- is the owner involved?
- how long has the business been around? 
- what schools are you involved with? 
- if you had to sort these in order, what would they be for you at the moment?  growth, admin workload reduction, quality improvement

What's the plan? 
Build a standard database package for extra-curricular providers that replaces the need for spreadsheets and file-sharing systems. 

What does it cost? 
$50/tutor/month + 1.8% per enrolment.

What if they say they're ready to go? 
We're building our list of day-one clients. 
It'll take us 10-20 full-time weeks to get something working. 
We know you can't give us a commitment. 
But we're confident that when we're ready to go, you'll want to migrate.  
So can we keep you in the loop? 

How are you going to build it? 
Venture funding. 

We'd have to have a very good relationship with our first few clients, because things will change for them.
