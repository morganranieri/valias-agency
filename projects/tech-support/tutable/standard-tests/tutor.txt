Feature: Office support for the tutor

  Background: 
    Given I am logged in as a tutor 

  Scenario: Tutor can see their dashboard
    When I visit the tenant homepage
    Then I am redirected to the dashboard

  Scenario: Tutor can see their programs 
    Given I am on the dashboard
    And I have been assigned to a at least one program
    Then I can see my program(s)
    
  Scenario: Tutor can mark the roll 
    Given I have clicked a program
    And I have clicked a session number
    And I have students in the program
    Then I should be able to mark them as absent/present/unrecorded

  Scenario: Tutor can find students to add to their group
    Given a guardian has previously added a student in the same school
    And the student is eligible for the program
    And I am in a program session page
    When I click add a student under a group heading
    Then I should be able to search for eligible students to add to the group
  
  Scenario: Tutor adds a previously registered attendee to their group
    Given I am looking at a list of eligible students to add to my group
    When I add the student
    And they appear on the roll
    Then the guardian should see a pending enrolment on their dashboard
 
  Scenario: Tutor can move students to another group in the same program
    Given I am on the program session page
    And there is more than one group in the program
    When I click then pencil icon
    Then I should be able to assign them to another group even if I am not assigned to that group
