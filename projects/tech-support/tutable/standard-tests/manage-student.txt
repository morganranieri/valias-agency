Feature: Guardian can manage a student

  Background:
    Given I am logged in as a guardian

  Scenario: Guardian adds a student
    Given I am on the dashboard
    When I click add a student
    And I fill in the student name, school and grade
    And I click submit
    Then I should see the student's name on my dashboard

  Scenario: Guardian updates student name
    Given I am on the dashboard
    When I click edit next to my student's name
    And I update their name
    Then I should see their updated name on my dashboard

  Scenario: Guardian updates multiple student profile fields at once
    Given I am on the dashboard 
    When I click edit next to my student's name
    And I update their grade and school and "medical information"
    And I am redirected to the dashboard
    And I click edit next to their name again
    Then I should see their updated information

  Scenario: Remove (not supported)
