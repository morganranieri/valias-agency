Feature: Guardian's enroll students

  Background:
    I am logged in as a guardian
    And I have a student
    And the student has a school
    And the student has a grade/year
    And there are programs they are eligible for

  Scenario: See the programs 
    Given I am on the dashboard
    When I click enroll next to my student
    Then I should see all the eligible programs   

  Scenario: Confirm the programs 
    Given I have clicked on an eligible program
    Then I should see more details with a program enrolment confirmation 

  Scenario: Enroll in a program
    Given I can see more details with a program enrolment confirmation
    When I click on confirm enrollment     
    And complete payment
    Then I should receive a confirmation email 
      
  Scenario: Adjust the number of sessions
    Given the tenant permits adjustments
    When I add a session adjustment 
    Then the total price should be adjusted
