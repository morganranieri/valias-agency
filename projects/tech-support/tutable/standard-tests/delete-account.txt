Feature: Deleting data and accounts

  Background:


  Scenario: Guardian requests account deletion
    Given I am logged in
    And I am a guardian 
    And I am not any other type of user 
    When I request an account deletion
    Then I should be logged out 

  Scenario: Deleted guardian signs up again
    Given I am on the login page
    When I sign up with the same email as an account that has been deleted
    Then I should not see any information that was associated with the deleted account

  Scenario: Administrator removes a tutor's access
    Given I am logged in as an administrator 
    When I remove a tutor 
    Then the tutor should not be able to login

  Scenario: Tutor's access has been removed but I still want to see their attendance records
    Given I am logged in as an administrator
    When I have removed a tutor
    Then I should still see their attendance records 
    
  Scenario: Administrator removes a school officer
    Given I am logged in as an administrator
    When I remove a school officer
    Then the school officer should not be able to login

  Scenario: School officer removes another school officer
    Given I am logged in as a school officer
    And there is another school officer at the same school
    When I remove the other school officer 
    Then the other school officer should not be able to login

  Scenario: Tenant Administrator requests deletion of their whole setup
    Manual process

  Scenario: Tutor requests account deletion (not supported)
