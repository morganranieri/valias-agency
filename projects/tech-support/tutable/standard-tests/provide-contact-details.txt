Feature: User provides their contact details

  Background: 

  Scenario: Guardian adds contact details during signup 
    Given I have clicked sign up from the tenant homepage
    When I give my full name
    And phone number
    And email address
    And click next
    Then I should be see "add a student"

  Scenario: Tutor/"school officer"/administrator adds contact details post signup
    Given I am logged in as tutor/"school officer"/administrator
    And I have not provided my contact details
    And I have selected "provide my contact details" from the dashboard
    When I have provided new contact details
    And submitted the form
    Then I should see the new details confirmed on the dashboard

  Scenario: A user with contact details updates their contact details
    Given I am logged in as tutor/"school officer"/administrator
    And I have provided my contact details
    And I have selected "update my contact details" from the dashboard
    When I have provided updated contact details
    And submitted the form
    Then I should see the updated details confirmed on the dashboard
